# Privacy Policy

This policy covers **Ashen Build** for iPhone, made by Corvin Ghita.
Last updated: 22 November 2026.

## The short version

The app collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

## What the app stores

- The builds you create (class, stats, weapons, armor, rings) are saved only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage.
- A few settings (appearance, sort order, the last section you opened) are saved on your iPhone as well.
- Deleting a build removes it; deleting the app removes everything.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the app does.
